# report

Collects the six numbers defined on <https://atlas.smolevich.com/analytics/what-to-measure/>, compares
them with the previous period, and ends with three conclusions and one action.

**This skill works only from files.** The author downloads the exports and uploads them here. It has
no access to their Google account and never asks for one.

<!-- replace: ## Append to the series -->
## Append to the series

One snapshot a week, same weekday, same window. Give the author the block as markdown to append to
their history file.

A number you cannot compare with itself last month is decoration. One reading is not a trend, and two
are barely a line.
