One case is up: [this atlas, two days in](/cases/atlas-itself/). It is the site you are reading, measured on itself.

This page is the bar the next one has to clear, and the form to send it in.

## What a case has to contain

- **A starting point.** Where the project stood before: traffic, signups, revenue. Zero is fine — zero is usually the honest answer.
- **Numbers with a date and a source.** Which panel each figure was read off, and on what day you read it.
- **What did not work.** The dead ends, in the order you walked them. Without those I have no way to tell a case from an ad, and neither has anybody else.
- **The move you would not repeat** — the one that worked once because of the timing, or a launch, or somebody's mood, and will not work a second time.
- **A link to the project**, so the reader can open it and check.

## What does not go up

- A launch announcement. "We are live" is news, and I have nowhere to put news.
- A growth chart with no dates and no scale on the axis.
- A ratio with no base under it. Tripled from what, over how long.
- Advice with no project behind it — if you did not run it, nobody can check it.

## How to send yours

[Open the case template](https://github.com/Smolevich/pet-project-atlas/issues/new?template=share-case.yml). It asks for the project, the numbers with a source for each, and the dead ends. It takes about ten minutes if you have the dashboards open.

Your voice stays as you wrote it. Guest pages carry `voice: guest` in frontmatter and are not rewritten into the house voice of the atlas, though the facts get checked exactly as they are everywhere else here.

The terms are in [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/Smolevich/pet-project-atlas/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#guest-cases): `CC BY 4.0`, your byline, a link to your project.

## Why there is only one

The rest of my numbers are still coming in, and I would rather publish them a month late than round them off into something that reads well. A case written before the data arrives breaks the first rule on this page.

Until the next one lands, the measurements live on the pages that produced them, section by section along [the route](/start/).
