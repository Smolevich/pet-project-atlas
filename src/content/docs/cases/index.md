One case is up: [this atlas, two days in](/cases/atlas-itself/). It is the site you are reading, measured on itself.

This page is the bar the next one has to clear. It says what a case has to contain, and how to send one.

## What a case has to contain

- **A starting point.** Where the project stood before: traffic, signups, revenue. Zero is fine.
- **Numbers with a date and a source.** Which panel each figure was read off, and on what day.
- **What did not work.** The dead ends, in order. A case without them is an ad.
- **The move you would not repeat.** The thing that worked once and will not work twice.
- **A link to the project.** The reader has to be able to open it and check.

## What does not go up

- A launch announcement. "We are live" is news, not a case.
- A growth chart with no dates and no scale on the axis.
- A ratio with no base under it. "Traffic tripled" is a shape, not a measurement.
- Advice with no project behind it. Second-hand tips belong in someone else's blog post.

## How to send yours

[Open the case template](https://github.com/Smolevich/pet-project-atlas/issues/new?template=share-case.yml). It asks for the project, the numbers with a source for each, and the dead ends. It takes about ten minutes if you have the dashboards open.

Your voice stays as you wrote it. Guest pages carry `voice: guest` in frontmatter and are not rewritten into the house voice of the atlas. Facts are checked exactly as they are everywhere else here.

The terms are in [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/Smolevich/pet-project-atlas/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#guest-cases): `CC BY 4.0`, your byline, a link to your project.

## Why there is only one

The rest of my numbers are still being collected, and I would rather publish them late than round them. Writing a case before the data is in would break the first rule on this page.

Until the next one lands, the measurements live on the pages that produced them, section by section along [the route](/start/).
