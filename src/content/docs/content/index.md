description: Nobody searches your product by name yet. What to write instead, and what shape to write it in.
---

Nobody types your product's name into a search box yet. The demand that exists is worded as a problem, in someone else's vocabulary, spread across dozens of phrasings that all want the same answer.

Two pages here. [What to write about when nobody searches your name](/content/keyword-clusters/) is the plan: collect the real phrasings, group them into clusters, give each cluster one page, then decide the order.

[What a page that ranks and gets cited is made of](/content/page-templates/) is the shape of the page itself, including the failures block — the one nobody can copy off you.

Do the plan first. I spent a month writing pages around my own word for the feature, and it was a good word. Nobody typed it.
