Nobody types your product's name into a search box yet. People search for their problem, in their own words. They ask for the same answer in a dozen different phrasings.

Two pages here.

[What to write about when nobody searches your name](/content/keyword-clusters/) is the plan. You collect the real phrasings and group them into clusters — sets of phrases that all want one answer. You give each cluster one page. You decide the order of the pages last.

[What a page that ranks and gets cited is made of](/content/page-templates/) is the shape of the page itself. It includes the failures block, and that is the part nobody can copy off you.

Do the plan first. I spent a month writing pages around my own word for the feature. It was a good word. Nobody typed it.
