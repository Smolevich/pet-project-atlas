Nobody types your product's name into a search box yet. The demand that exists is worded as a problem, in someone else's vocabulary, spread across dozens of phrasings.

Two pages here. [What to write about when nobody searches your name](/content/keyword-clusters/) is the plan. Collect the real phrasings, group them into clusters, give each cluster one page, then decide the order.

[What a page that ranks and gets cited is made of](/content/page-templates/) is the shape of the page itself. That includes the block most people skip.

Plan first. A well-built page aimed at a query nobody types is still nothing.
