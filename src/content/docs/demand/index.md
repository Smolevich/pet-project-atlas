description: Before anything else — does anyone type these words, and does that audience pay for anything. Where the real phrasings come from, and what the paid panels are actually measuring.

You can be perfectly indexed for a phrase nobody types. I know, because I was.

That is why this section stands first. Everything after it costs weeks — crawler access, pages, links, dashboards all pay only if somebody is already searching.

Two questions live here, and a phrase can pass one and fail the other. [The words people actually type](/demand/how-people-search/) asks whether anyone searches at all. [Whether that audience pays for anything](/demand/will-they-pay/) asks whether there is money behind the searching.

That second answer is the one that stings. Real volume, an empty ad block, everybody happily finding the free way. That combination is cheap to catch now and expensive later.

[Which market to build for](/demand/pick-a-market/) is both questions worked through on real figures. Three niches and four markets, read in Semrush on one day. The niche with the biggest number turned out to be the worst of the three.

The paid tools live here rather than further along, because this is the moment people buy them. A panel describes a market you have not entered. It describes a site with no traffic far worse, and that is exactly the case I bought one for.

Do both before the writing. A well-built page aimed at a phrase you invented is a month spent on your own vocabulary.
