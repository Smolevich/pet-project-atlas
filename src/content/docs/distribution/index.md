You posted the launch. The graph spiked for a day, and the next day it was flat again.

Nobody links to you, because nobody has heard of the project, and the pages ranking above you belong to other people.

Three pages here.

[Where the first external links come from](/distribution/catalogs/) covers the kinds of venue there are, the card you write once and reuse, and how to tell a live directory from an abandoned one.

[Communities and forums](/distribution/communities/) is about the places where those shortlists get written: what gets a post removed, and why you open the account long before you first mention the product. I have not run a campaign there. That page says so in its first lines.

[Search inside the platform, not only in Google](/distribution/in-platform-visibility/) is about the separate index inside Telegram, app stores and marketplaces. A name in the wrong script hides you there completely.

None of this work happens on your own site. It is slow. It is far less satisfying than editing a page you control, but it is also what moves a project that is technically clean and still invisible.
