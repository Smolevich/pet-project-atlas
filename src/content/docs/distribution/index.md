You posted the launch, the graph spiked for a day, and the next day it was flat again. Nobody links to a project nobody has heard of, and the pages ranking above you belong to other people.

Two pages here. [Where the first external links come from](/distribution/catalogs/) covers the kinds of venue and the card you prepare once. It also covers how to tell a live directory from an abandoned one.

[Search inside the platform, not only in Google](/distribution/in-platform-visibility/) is about the separate index inside Telegram, app stores and marketplaces. A name in the wrong script hides you there completely.

Both are off-site work. It is slower than editing your own pages. It is also what moves a project that is technically clean and still invisible.
