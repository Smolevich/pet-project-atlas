You posted the launch, the graph spiked for a day, and the next day it was flat again. Nobody links to a project nobody has heard of, and the pages ranking above you belong to other people.

Three pages here. [Where the first external links come from](/distribution/catalogs/) covers the kinds of venue, the card you prepare once, and how to tell a live directory from an abandoned one.

[Communities and forums](/distribution/communities/) is about the venues where those shortlists actually get written: what gets a post removed, and why the account comes before the first mention. I have not run a campaign there, and that page says so in its first lines.

[Search inside the platform, not only in Google](/distribution/in-platform-visibility/) is about the separate index inside Telegram, app stores and marketplaces, where a name in the wrong script hides you completely.

All three are work outside your own domain, which is slower and far less satisfying than editing a page you control. It is also the part that moves a project that is technically clean and still invisible.
