Someone describes your problem to an assistant and gets a shortlist without you on it. A search page has ten slots and a scroll. An answer has room for a few sources, so being absent is the default outcome.

Three pages here, and they run in that order. [AI crawlers and llms.txt](/geo/llms-txt-and-crawlers/) is about access: which agents exist and who owns them. It also covers where a block actually lives, and what belongs in `llms.txt`.

[Why AI answers cite someone else](/geo/citable-pages/) is about the writing. Answer first, facts with dates, one checkable claim that is yours alone.

[Who actually crawls you](/geo/who-actually-crawls-you/) is the log behind both. Which agents came, what they fetched, and how rarely anybody asked for `llms.txt`.

Access before content. A blocked crawler means absence from the answer no matter how good the page is.
