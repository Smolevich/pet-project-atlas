Someone describes your problem to an assistant and gets a shortlist without you on it. A search page has ten slots and a scroll; an answer has room for a few sources, so being absent is the default outcome.

Two pages here, and they run in that order. [AI crawlers and llms.txt](/geo/llms-txt-and-crawlers/) is about access — which agents exist, who owns them, where a block actually lives, and what belongs in `llms.txt`. [Why AI answers cite someone else](/geo/citable-pages/) is about the writing: answer first, facts with dates, one checkable claim that is yours alone.

Access before content. A blocked crawler means absence from the answer no matter how good the page is.
