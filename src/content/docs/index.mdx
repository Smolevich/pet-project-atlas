## I am not an SEO expert

I build side projects and I read their logs. Here I show what I saw in my own dashboards, panels and access logs, and how I went looking.

Your data will say something else. Copy the procedure, not my numbers, because my numbers are one small project on the day I measured it.

Sometimes the number is embarrassing. One click in three months. Not one AI agent ever fetched the file I told people to publish, and the mysterious crawler in my log turned out to be my own `curl`.

Those numbers are on the pages too, because if you show a reader only the wins, he never learns where the walls are.

## What every page owes you

Every page here hands you something you can run: a command, a query, a panel to open — not a principle to consider.

Every number carries the day I measured it and the panel I read it off. Where I have neither, I take the number out of the page, and I do not round it into a hedge, because a hedge looks like data and is not.

At the bottom of every page sits a block called "What did not work". It is never empty. The build refuses a page where it is.

Two sections sit outside the route. [Tools](/tools/) says which skill to run for which symptom, and [Cases](/cases/) holds the bar a full write-up has to clear. One case is up so far — this site, measured on itself.
