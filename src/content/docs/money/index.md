---
title: 7. Money
description: People use it for free and you do not know what to charge. What one user costs when the product calls an AI, and how many payers close the month.
---

People use the thing, nobody pays, and you cannot say whether any price would cover the bills. If the product makes AI calls on the user's behalf, every action already has a cost attached to it.

One page here. [What one user costs](/money/unit-economics/) covers variable cost per action and the fixed cost of a month with no users. Break-even is expressed in payers instead of percentages.

It also covers measuring the cost of an action rather than estimating it. That is the difference between a model and a hope.

The usual finding at this size is that the threshold is tiny and the payers are missing. That points back at demand and activation, not at another round of cost cutting.
